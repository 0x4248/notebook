# The 0x4248 Journal of Applied Systems and Network Engineering

***
![[jasne-cover-s.png|300]]

The 0x4248 Journal of Applied Systems and Network Engineering (JASNE) is an open access research notebook publishing practical and applied notes on the design, administration, and troubleshooting of computer systems and networks. It serves as a running record of technical knowledge, configurations, and lessons learned across operating systems, networking, and infrastructure work.
