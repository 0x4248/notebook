## The issue with superintelligence (ASI)
Firstly what is ASI? Artificial Superintelligence doesn't exist *yet* — it's the hypothetical form of artificial intelligence that surpasses the best human intelligence across virtually all relevant intellectual domains, including scientific reasoning, creativity, strategic planning and problem-solving. So are we there yet? *No*, not quite we aren't even at AGI yet.

**Though several US politicians call it ASI already.** (*Even though It's not)*

### What really classes as AI, AGI and ASI
To better explain this, we can use a table:

| Capability          | Current frontier AI (GPT, Claude, Grok)  | AGI                             | ASI                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Reasoning           | Advanced but inconsistent                | Human-level / Slightly better   | Highly advanced and beyond humans            |
| Learning new things | Limited                                  | Broad                           | Potentially extraordinary                    |
| Scientific research | Can assist but can't research new things | Potentially research on its own | Likely to surpass humanity                   |
| Problem solving     | Inconsistent and uneven                  | Broad human level               | Superhuman / Surpasses human problem solving |
| Autonomy            | Somewhat there                           | Extensive                       | Very extensive                               |
| Self improving      | Limited                                  | Capable                         | Recursive                                    |
Ok, but why isn't models today AGI or even ASI?
- Models have limitations on long-term autonomous execution
- Requires human content to learn new things and can't self-learn
- Struggles with reliability and accuracy (though this is getting better and better every year)

## Videos can no longer be proof something happened
**Jean Phil** is a completely artificially generated character that fooled a lot of people thinking this person with an interesting haircut was real. This was done using a reference video and then getting AI to replace the person in the video with the made up character. The thing that made everyone believe it was real was the amount of detail, physics (in the hair) and realism it had.
