# Who is Blix

***
Who is this Blix person whos been roaming the internet? Well its me — more specifically *Blix* is my fursona.

![[blix-ref-sheet.png]]

**The image above shows my reference sheet.**

Blix is an anthropomorphic folf (fox-wolf hybrid) and my personal fursona. The character features blue, white, and black fur. 

The name "Blix" comes from the dominant blue ("Bl") plus the "x", which is derived from my username 0x4248. As for the "**I**" in the name — well, it's just *there*. Deal with it.

I'm not making this up, but my brain went like this thinking of a name:
- Blue fox
- Bluewolf??
- *How about folf* — Bluefolf... Nope, still bad
- Blux... *French???*
- Bluix... *French-er??*
- Blix... Wait that works, yeah... **Blix**!

## But why be a furry?
Many people online know the furry fandom and a lot of negative things have been said about it — this article is not about clearing up misconceptions however I'm going to explain some things. 

**No, furry doesn't mean**:
- I act like an animal
- I want to be treated like an animal
- I want to do nothing other than be an animal

Like anyone else — I'm an **ordinary person**. In fact It's extremely likely you have walked past **many** furries; *without even knowing or batting an eye*!
### Ok but what even is the appeal and what makes you want to become part of the community. 
Well, this is a question I get asked and honesty it's the people that make it special. It's not about the art, fur suits and anything else — it's the people that make it special.
