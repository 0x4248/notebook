# The Sun

***

The Sun (*Sol*) is a [[star]] that is at the center of the [[Solar System]] and is a giant sphere of [[plasma]]. The Sun is 8.3 light minutes away from [[Earth]] but the next nearest star [[Proxima Centauri]] takes longer than four years to reach us. The Sun is huge — 100 Earths in diameter, its also 300,000 times more dense. The Sun consists of the two lightest elements in the Solar System: Hydrogen and Helium.
