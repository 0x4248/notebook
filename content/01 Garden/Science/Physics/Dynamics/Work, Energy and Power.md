# Work, Energy and Power

***
Work is the force (*f*) applied to an object to move an object over a specified distance (*d*). Work can be calculated by:
$$
W = fd
$$
Work and energy are very similar. When you move an object and apply work to it, you change the objects' energy. Energy can be defined as the ability to do work.
